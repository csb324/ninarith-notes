Copper hair, long blue overcoat. Coppery scales on his face. Copper scales everywhere, because he's a copper dragon

Carries a black onyx and obsidian gun. The hilt comes to almost a red ruby point. Can stab from the hilt, it shoots glass bullets that explode on impact. It's a family weapon from [[Victoria Several]], a blood pistol, NO ONE ELSE should touch it once he's stabbed himself with it. It's cursed, but only sometimes. He's maybe got something ~special~ building with Victoria. (He denies this. Apparently there's no way Victoria's interested, per [[Magister]]). 

Stayed at the Goldhide resort while in [[Jalantar]]. Here for the [[Bellisme Show]], and is building up for some big business with his group.

Poisoned [[Adrienne Spout|Adrienne]], leading to long term digestive problems. [[Freya]] got him back pretty good though. 

He and [[Rez]] have pioneered the "Two string method".
